# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free research and engineering
community. Be considerate, discuss technical disagreements with evidence, and
avoid discriminatory, threatening, or personally identifying content.

Project maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation when
behavior is unsafe or persistently disruptive. Report conduct concerns
privately to the maintainers through the repository's security contact. Every
report will be reviewed in good faith and handled as confidentially as
practical.
